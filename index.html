A UK drill beat made elsewhere, dropped into SonorStudio with its kit. Tempo, patterns, 808 notes and mixer come with it.

Four windows, and the shortcuts you already use

F6 Channel rack
One row per sound. Paint steps, mute, solo, and send each channel to the mixer.
F7 Piano roll
Draw, move and stretch notes, with the other channels as ghost notes and a velocity lane.
F5 Playlist
Arrange your patterns into a song across 32 tracks, then press L to play it.
F9 Mixer
24 inserts with EQ, filter, drive, compressor, delay, reverb and limiter.

Bring your projects and your sounds

  1. Drop a .flp

    Tempo, channels, patterns, playlist and mixer come in. Plugins that only exist on your computer are listed, not guessed.

  2. Drop your kit

    A folder or a .zip. Each channel finds its sample by file name, and plays a built-in sound until then.

  3. Press Space

    It plays. Export a WAV, or a project file with its sounds inside to open it anywhere.

Free, and it stays free.

SonorStudio is in open beta: some things are still missing, nothing is locked. Your sounds and projects stay in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
